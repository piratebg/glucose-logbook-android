# Safety and intended use

This experimental community project provides informational glucose record keeping and descriptive reports. It has no claimed clinical certification, regulatory authorization, diagnostic accuracy or treatment benefit. It does not recommend medication, calculate insulin doses, diagnose diabetes, predict events or provide emergency monitoring.

Verify imported readings, units and timestamps against the original meter. Incorrect meter clocks, timezone changes, incomplete imports, communication failures and software defects can affect records. A successful sync does not prove that every relevant measurement was captured. Do not delay care while waiting for the app or use it as the sole basis for treatment decisions.

Reports describe intermittent recorded measurements. Unmeasured periods are unknown; percentages are counts of readings, not continuous glucose monitoring time in range. User-configured targets and descriptive repeat patterns are not personalized clinical advice. Review reports and targets with a qualified care professional.

Only one meter model has community-reported testing. Review docs/COMPATIBILITY.md before relying on an import workflow. Phone power management and Bluetooth conditions can interrupt communication. Keep the meter's own records and verify exports.

Engineering tests are not clinical validation. Intended use, claims and distribution context can affect medical-device and other obligations. A disclaimer does not itself determine regulatory classification. Obtain qualified advice before clinical, commercial or broader deployment.
